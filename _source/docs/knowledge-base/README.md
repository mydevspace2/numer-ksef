## The 10 most important lessons (short version)
1. **Pick a small, real problem.** New laws are great because they create new searches.
2. **Use the exact words people type** (Google autocomplete, Ahrefs free tools, SBMM).
3. **Look at the competition yourself.** If 8 or more free tools are on Google's pages 1–2 and most get 0 traffic, **don't start a new site there**. Add the tool to a site you already have.
4. **One page per keyword**, the tool at the top, and 800–1,200+ useful words with tables and a FAQ under it.
5. **Make it fast** (our KSeF site scored 98 mobile and 100 desktop on PageSpeed) and mobile-friendly.
6. **Tell Google and Bing:** Search Console, sitemap, IndexNow. New sites often get "Quota exceeded" on Request Indexing, so just retry another day.
7. **Write 1 helpful blog post a day** for the first month.
8. **Promote carefully:** one honest post at a time, and only with your OK. At most 1 new link per site per month.
9. **AdSense only after about 3 months** of real visitors.
10. **Be patient:** ranking takes 2–6 months, and only 2–3 out of 10 sites usually work. Keep each site at least a year.
